Privacy note — “See it in your room”

How Gruha handles the photo you upload. Consent version 2026-10-09.v1

What we take

Only the room photo you choose to upload, and the preview image we generate from it. We don’t ask for your name, email or address to make a preview.

What we do with it

Your photo is sent to our AI partner, Google Gemini, purely to generate the preview showing the product in your room. That is the only purpose.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not use your photo for marketing.
  • We do not sell or share it with anyone other than the AI partner above.
  • We do not publish it — stored images are private and never publicly accessible.

How long we keep it

Your uploaded photo and the generated preview are stored privately for up to 90 days, so the shop can show you the result and we can investigate problems. After that they are deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

Ticking the consent box is entirely up to you — nothing is uploaded until you do, and you can close the panel at any point. To ask for your images to be deleted, contact the shop you used this on and reference the date and product; they can reach Gruha to action it.
