Privacy note — “See it in your room”

How Gruha handles the photo you upload. Consent version 2026-10-09.v1

What we store

Only the room photo you choose to upload, and the preview image we generate from it. We do not ask for your name, address or payment details to make a preview.

Who processes it

Your photo is sent to our AI partner, Google Gemini, solely to generate the preview showing the product in your room. That is the only purpose it is used for.

How long we keep it

WhatKept forWhy
Your room photo and the generated preview 90 days, then automatically deleted So the shop can show you the result and we can investigate problems
A usage record (date, product, whether the render succeeded) — no image Kept indefinitely It is the shop’s billing and usage record

Deletion at 90 days runs automatically every day — it is enforced by a scheduled job, not just a promise. After it runs, the usage record remains but the images are gone.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not use your photo for marketing.
  • We do not use it to train AI models.
  • We do not sell or share it with anyone besides the AI partner above.
  • Stored images are private — never publicly accessible, and only ever served over short-lived signed links to the shop that generated them.

Your choices

Consent is entirely up to you: nothing is uploaded until you tick the box, and you can close the panel at any time. To ask for your images to be deleted sooner, email us at hello.gruhalabs@gmail.com — tell us roughly when you used it and which shop, and we will remove them.
