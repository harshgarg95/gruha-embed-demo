An alternative to AR for furniture websites in India

Last updated 14 November 2026

A photo-based room render is an alternative to augmented reality (AR) for furniture websites: instead of placing a 3D model through the phone camera, it renders your product into a photo of the shopper’s own room. Gruha works this way. It is not AR.

In short

  • AR needs a 3D model of every product and a phone that supports AR.
  • A photo-based render needs your catalogue photo and one photo of the shopper’s room.
  • AR gives a live view; a render gives one still image of their own room, with an itemised quote in ₹.
  • Each suits different needs; the trade-offs are set out below.

What AR needs

An AR viewer places a 3D model of the product into the live camera view on the shopper’s phone. That means a 3D model for every product, made and kept up to date per SKU, and a phone that supports AR.

What a photo-based room render needs instead

A photo-based render uses two pictures: your existing catalogue photograph of the product, and a photo the shopper takes of their own room. The product is rendered into the room photo at its real size, lit by the light already in that room. No 3D models are needed.

If you need a live view the shopper can walk around, AR does that and a render does not. If you have a large catalogue and no 3D models, a photo-based render works from what you already have, and Gruha’s comes with an itemised quote in ₹ and a buy link.

How Gruha works

  1. One button on your product page. It is switched on from your theme editor, and nothing in your theme is edited.
  2. The shopper uploads a photo of the room they are buying for, bare or already lived-in, from the phone camera or gallery.
  3. Your product appears in it at its real size, lit by the light already in that room. It takes about a minute.
  4. An itemised quote in ₹, with a buy link straight to your product page.

Gruha is made in Hyderabad for furniture and décor brands. It is a visualisation, not a photograph: a daylit indoor photo gives a much better result than a dim or outdoor one. See what it can and can’t do.

Questions

Is Gruha AR?

No. Gruha is an AI render of a photo of the shopper’s own room, not augmented reality. There is no live camera view and no 3D model.

Do I need 3D models?

No. It runs on your existing catalogue photos: each render is generated from your catalogue photograph and the shopper’s room photograph.

Does the shopper need to download an app?

No. The button sits on your product page and works in the shopper’s mobile browser, with no app and no login.
