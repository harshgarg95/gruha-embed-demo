A “See it in your room” button for Shopify furniture stores

Last updated 14 November 2026

A “See it in your room” button lets a shopper on your Shopify product page upload a photo of their own room and see your product in it, at its real size. Gruha is that button for furniture and décor brands, with an itemised quote in ₹.

In short

  • On Shopify, Gruha is an app switched on from your theme editor.
  • Nothing in your theme is edited, and it comes off just as easily.
  • The shopper gets a render of their own room and an itemised ₹ quote with a buy link to your existing product page.
  • On WooCommerce or any other site, it is one script tag.

How it works on a Shopify product page

  1. One button on your product page. It is switched on from your theme editor, and nothing in your theme is edited.
  2. The shopper uploads a photo of the room they are buying for, bare or already lived-in, from the phone camera or gallery.
  3. Your product appears in it at its real size, lit by the light already in that room. It takes about a minute.
  4. An itemised quote in ₹, with a buy link straight to your product page.

How we start

  1. We set up your catalogue and test the renders on real rooms before anything goes live.
  2. You try it yourself on your own living room, and tell us what you think.
  3. We switch it on in your theme editor. Nothing in your theme is edited, and it comes off just as easily.

Questions

Will it change my store’s design?

No. Nothing in your theme is edited, and it comes off just as easily.

Does it work on Shopify and other websites?

Yes. On Shopify it is an app you switch on from your theme editor. On WooCommerce or any other site it is one script tag.

Does it work on WhatsApp for my sales team?

Yes. Your sales team can send a finished render and a priced quote back on WhatsApp, instead of a catalogue link. Shoppers can also share their own render on WhatsApp.
