How to show furniture in a customer’s own room, on your product page

Last updated 14 November 2026

Showing furniture in a customer’s own room means letting a shopper see your product inside their room, not a studio, before they buy. Brands in India do this in three main ways, and each asks different things of the brand and the shopper.

In short

  • Lifestyle photos show your product in a styled room, but not in the shopper’s room.
  • 3D and AR viewers need a 3D model of every product.
  • Photo-based room renders use your existing catalogue photo and a photo of the shopper’s room.
  • Gruha is a photo-based room render: one button on your product page, an itemised quote in ₹.

Why a product shot is not enough

A white-background product shot tells a shopper what a piece looks like. It does not tell them whether it fits, whether it suits the room, or what it costs once it is in there. Dimensions in centimetres are not a picture of their hallway.

The three ways brands do it

1. Lifestyle photos

The brand stages the product in a styled room and photographs it. The shopper sees the piece in a room, but it is the brand’s room, with its light, floor and walls, not theirs.

2. 3D and AR viewers

The shopper views a 3D model of the product, or places it through the phone camera using augmented reality. This needs a 3D model of every product, made and kept up to date per SKU, and AR needs a phone that supports it.

3. Photo-based room renders

The shopper uploads a photo of their own room, and the product is rendered into that photo. It runs on the catalogue photos a brand already has, so no 3D models are needed. The result is a still image, not a live view.

How Gruha does it

Gruha is a “See it in your room” button for furniture and décor brands, made in Hyderabad. It is a photo-based room render, not AR.

  1. One button on your product page. It is switched on from your theme editor, and nothing in your theme is edited.
  2. The shopper uploads a photo of the room they are buying for, bare or already lived-in, from the phone camera or gallery.
  3. Your product appears in it at its real size, lit by the light already in that room. It takes about a minute.
  4. An itemised quote in ₹, with a buy link straight to your product page.

Several products can be placed in one room, with colour and fabric variants. Shoppers can share the render on WhatsApp or download it. On Shopify it is an app switched on from the theme editor; on WooCommerce or any other site it is one script tag.

It is a visualisation, not a photograph, and results vary with the room. See what it can and can’t do.

Questions

Do I need 3D models to show furniture in a customer’s room?

Not with a photo-based room render. Gruha runs on your existing catalogue photos: each render is generated from your catalogue photograph and the shopper’s room photograph.

Does the shopper need to download an app?

No. The button sits on your product page and works in the shopper’s mobile browser, with no app and no login.

How long does a render take?

About a minute, from the shopper’s phone photo to a priced room.
